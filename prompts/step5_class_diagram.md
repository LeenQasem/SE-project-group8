## Modeling decisions

- `User` is abstract, with `Administrator`, `EventOrganizer`, and `Volunteer` subclasses.
- Applications each belong to exactly one volunteer and one event; events have one organizer. Each task belongs to one event and is assigned to one volunteer. The requirement that assignments go to approved volunteers is stated in a note; no unrequested Application-to-Task link is invented.
- Attendance is associated with exactly one volunteer and one event, matching `uml/class_diagram.puml`; it is also associated with one recording organizer. Reports are generated by one user and may be event-specific or platform-wide.
- Types are technology-neutral. Status/report enumerations and event/task values are explicit model suggestions where requirements do not define allowed values; the existing architecture says the detailed schema remains undecided.
